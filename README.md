### 🙋 About Me

I work on **real-robot Embodied AI**: robot control, teleoperation and data collection, and deploying learned policies (VLA / IL) on physical hardware.

- 🏢 Previously Research Intern @ [Shanghai AI Laboratory](https://www.shlab.org.cn/) (Mar. 2025 – Sep. 2025)
- 🔧 Creator of [**control_your_robot**](https://github.com/Tian-Nian/control_your_robot): a unified pipeline for robot control, data collection, and policy deployment
- 🏆 Core lead author of [**XPolicyLab**](https://github.com/XPolicyLab/XPolicyLab), which won the **Best Tool Paper Award** at the IROS 2026 ScaleInfra Workshop
- 🤝 Contributor to [**RoboTwin 2.0**](https://github.com/RoboTwin-Platform/RoboTwin) (ICML 2026)
